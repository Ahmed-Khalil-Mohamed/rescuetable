# Visual assets

`public/brand-scene.png` is an AI-generated editorial illustration, not a photograph of actual customers or a product screenshot. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool.

Prompt:

Create a wide 16:9 editorial food photograph for a surplus-food sharing website. A reusable crate containing fresh bread loaves in kraft paper beside leafy vegetables, tomatoes and carrots on a warm stone community kitchen counter. Honest natural daylight, beautiful imperfect real ingredients, dark forest-green cloth, warm cream setting, overhead three-quarter framing. No people, no labels, no words, no logos or interface. Inviting, responsible, practical food collection image.

Brand icons are derived from the existing MIT-licensed Phosphor icon set; editable SVG sources and raster Windows icons are included in `public`.
